Premium Residential Sales

Residential Sales & Strategic Advisory

Navigating the Mumbai residential market requires more than just a listing; it requires a deep understanding of micro-market trends, upcoming infrastructure projects, and legal transparency. At Abhinav Estates, we specialize in high-ticket resale and primary sales across South Mumbai, the Western Suburbs, and the burgeoning Central corridors. Our approach is data-driven, ensuring that every recommendation we make is backed by current market valuations and future growth projections.

Our team conducts rigorous due diligence on every property, examining title deeds, occupation certificates, and society histories to ensure that our clients invest in assets that are not only beautiful but legally sound. Whether you are looking for a luxury sea-facing penthouse in Worli, a heritage apartment in Colaba, or a modern high-rise 3 BHK in Andheri West, our advisors provide a seamless, end-to-end transition—from the initial site visit and price negotiation to the final registration and handover of the keys.

Commercial & Corporate

Corporate Leasing & Commercial Spaces

We bridge the gap between multinational corporations and premium commercial infrastructure. In a city like Mumbai, where space is the ultimate luxury, our commercial division focuses on identifying office environments that align with your corporate identity, operational requirements, and employee well-being. We understand that your office is more than just a place to work; it is a statement of your brand’s prestige and a tool for productivity.

From Grade-A office towers in the Bandra-Kurla Complex (BKC) to boutique studio spaces in Lower Parel and IT parks in Powai, we handle the complex negotiations of lease renewals, lock-in periods, and fit-out contributions. Our extensive network allows us to access off-market listings that aren't available to the general public, giving your business a competitive edge. We ensure your business finds a home that fosters innovation while meticulously optimizing your real estate overheads and legal protections.

NRI Specialist

NRI Property Asset Management

For our clients residing overseas, distance should never be a barrier to profitable real estate ownership in India. We act as your dedicated local family office in Mumbai, managing your entire property portfolio with absolute trust, integrity, and efficiency. We recognize the unique challenges faced by NRIs, from navigating complex tax regulations to ensuring their properties are maintained and protected from encroachment or neglect.

Our comprehensive NRI services include finding verified corporate tenants, executing registered leave and license agreements, routine maintenance supervision, and assistance with property tax and utility compliance. We provide regular digital updates and walkthroughs, ensuring you have total visibility over your assets. We treat your property as our own, ensuring it remains a high-performing asset with maximum rental yield and capital appreciation while you focus on your life abroad.
